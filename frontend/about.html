THE PHILOSOPHY

ARCHITECTURAL STREETWEAR MADE FOR LONGEVITY

VYBE emerged from a frustration with flimsy fast-fashion graphics that lose their structure after two washes. We set out to engineer pieces that boast substantial physical presence: 450 GSM loopback cotton, 280 GSM drop-shoulder tees, and water-repellent tactical flight bombers.

Our design language marries Tokyo minimalism with raw London underground street cultures. Sizing is intentionally relaxed, boxy, and dropped at the shoulders so you look elevated without ever trying too hard.
